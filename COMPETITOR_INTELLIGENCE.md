## 3. Market Gap Identification

### Industry Flaws in Existing Solutions
1. **The "Single Score" Myth:** Commercial tools like Jobscan output a single percentage (e.g., "74% Match"), hiding whether the issue is missing hard skills, poor document layout, or lack of experience.
2. **AI Fabrication & Hallucination:** Tools like Rezi and Kickresume use unconstrained LLM prompts to write resume bullets, often inventing tools, metrics, or years of experience candidates do not possess.
3. **Disconnected Workflows:** Candidates must use Jobscan for matching, Teal for tracking, Simplify for autofill, and Final Round for interview prep—copy-pasting data across 4 platforms.
4. **Zero Evidence Traceability:** No existing product links candidate resume claims to verifiable proof (GitHub commits, project repos, portfolio links, course certificates).
5. **Interview Disconnect:** Tailored resumes often contain inflated buzzwords that candidates cannot defend during technical interviews. No tool currently checks **Resume-to-Interview Consistency**.
